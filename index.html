How to play

The idea

Eat four square meals a day and clock each one in when it actually happens. Log on time for full coins, log late for half, and let the day slip past midnight and that meal earns nothing — though you can still record it for your own history.

Meal windows

🥐 Breakfast4:00 AM – 11:59 AM
🥞 Brunch11:00 AM – 2:59 PM
🍛 Lunch2:00 PM – 5:59 PM
🍲 Dinner5:00 PM – 11:59 PM

Windows overlap on purpose — Brunch opens while Breakfast is still logging. You can't log a meal before its window opens.

Turn on Enable reminders (top of the app) to get a nudge 1 hour and 10 minutes before each meal's full-points deadline. These only arrive while the app is open on this device — closing it fully means no reminder, since that would need a server to send real push notifications.

Scoring a meal

  • Log it before the window listed above ends → 100% of that meal's 100 coins.
  • Log it after the window ends but before midnight → 50% of the coins.
  • Log it after midnight, the next day or later → 0 coins, recorded for the record only.

The daily bonus

The bonus is extra coins added on top of what you already earned from meals — it never takes points away.

  • Log all 4 meals in a day → earn a bonus equal to 100% of your meal points (×1) added on top.
  • Log exactly 3 meals → earn a bonus equal to 50% of your meal points (×0.5) added on top.
  • Log only 1 or 2 meals → no bonus (×0) — but you still keep every point you actually earned.

Example: log all 4 meals at full points (400 meal points) → +400 bonus (100%) → 800 for the day. Log only 3 at full points (300 meal points, since a 4th wasn't logged) → +150 bonus (50%) → 450 for the day.

Medication

Two check-ins, each worth the same 100 coins as a meal: Started meds and Completed meds. They're available all day, worth full coins if logged the same day, and don't affect the meal bonus.

Made a mistake?

Every logged meal or medication has an "Unlog" link. It asks you to confirm before removing the entry and its points — nothing is deleted by accident.

Performance

Rather than compare your real coin total to a big hypothetical number, the breakdown shows performance: the percentage of the day's (or your all-time) maximum possible points you've actually banked. Tap the ⓘ next to it for the exact numbers behind the percentage.

Leaderboard & history

Tap "Show leaderboard" to reveal today's rankings — it resets each morning, and the competition plays to win, so don't expect easy scores. Missed something entirely? Use "Back-log a missed entry" to keep a complete record of past days — those always score 0 and don't count toward that day's bonus.

Calendar

Tap "Show calendar" at the bottom to see every day on record, color-graded on a letter-grade scale (A–F, GPA 0.0–5.0) based on that day's performance. Switch how each day is labeled — raw score, percentage, letter grade, or grade point — with the buttons above the grid, and tap any colored day for its full breakdown. Grade points scale smoothly within each band (e.g. mid-band isn't the same as the top of the band), and your running average GPA across every day is shown in the Breakdown panel.
